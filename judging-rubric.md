# 🏆 Judging Rubric — Reachy Mini × 0G Hackathon

**Score each team out of 25.** Circle one number per row, add them up. ~2 minutes per team.

> **Gate (mandatory):** the project **must meaningfully use at least one of 0G Chain, Compute, or Storage.** No 0G integration = not eligible for prizes, regardless of score.

---

| # | Criterion | What you're judging | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **0G Integration** | Does it *really* use 0G Chain, Compute, or Storage — core to the project, not bolted on? | **/10** |
| 2 | **Reachy Mini Use** | Expressive, genuine use of the robot — movement, antennas, camera/mic/speakers, personality. | **/5** |
| 3 | **Creativity & Usefulness** | Is the idea original or genuinely useful? Would people want this? | **/5** |
| 4 | **Execution & Demo** | Did it actually work live? Was it explained clearly? | **/5** |
| | | **TOTAL** | **/25** |

---

## Quick scoring anchors

**1. 0G Integration — /10** *(the heart of the event)*
- **0** — no 0G used → **ineligible**
- **1–3** — token/superficial use (a single call, could be swapped out with no loss)
- **4–7** — solid, working use of one 0G service that matters to the project
- **8–10** — deep or multi-service use; 0G is essential to how it works (e.g. Compute for the robot's inference, Storage for its memory/behaviors, Chain for agent identity/ownership/payments)

**2. Reachy Mini Use — /5**
- **1–2** — robot barely moves / could be any screen app
- **3** — clear, intentional use of motion or sensing
- **4–5** — expressive and well-integrated; the *robot* is the point

**3. Creativity & Usefulness — /5**
- **1–2** — generic or unclear value · **3** — solid idea · **4–5** — original, memorable, or genuinely useful

**4. Execution & Demo — /5**
- **1–2** — broke or unclear · **3** — worked, explained okay · **4–5** — ran smoothly, told a tight story

---

## Tiebreaker
If totals are tied, the **higher 0G Integration score wins.** Still tied → judges' pick on overall impact.
